> **About this template**
>
> **Best for:** Recording what an iteration or stage will achieve, and what the team will improve afterwards.  
> **Use it when:** Work runs in repeating cycles (sprints, stages, milestones) and you want to learn between them.  
> **Skip it when:** One-off work with no cycles; use the final lessons learned instead.  
> **Why it matters:** Short loops of plan → do → reflect are the fastest way a team gets better; one improvement per cycle compounds.  
> **Variations by approach:** Scrum: sprint goal, sprint backlog, retrospective (timeboxes 8, 4 and 3 hours for a one-month sprint). Kanban: replenishment and improvement review. Lean: PDCA review. Predictive: stage-end review. Hybrid: retrospective per milestone.  
> **Professional references:** The Scrum Guide, November 2020 (Schwaber and Sutherland); The Kanban Guide, July 2020 (Kanban Guides)
