Alarms app: hourly sync no longer dies silently

- The x:05 / x:45 re-read is armed as an exact alarm (setExactAndAllowWhileIdle), with the relaxed one as fallback.
- It now runs in a short foreground service (SyncService, dataSync) right when the alarm fires, instead of going through JobScheduler, which Xiaomi / standby buckets postpone.
- The diary records when an automatic sync ran much later than planned or not at all.
