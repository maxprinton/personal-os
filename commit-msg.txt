Alarms app: hourly sync no longer dies silently + signs-of-life notification

- The x:05 / x:45 re-read is armed as an exact alarm (setExactAndAllowWhileIdle), with the relaxed one as fallback.
- It runs in a short foreground service (SyncService, dataSync) right when the alarm fires, instead of JobScheduler, which Xiaomi / standby buckets postpone.
- The diary records when an automatic sync ran much later than planned or not at all.
- New Heartbeat: one small silent notification (replaced each time) with the time, last sync result and next alarm. If its time stops moving, the phone put the app to sleep.
